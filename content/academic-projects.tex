\section{Selected Research \& Academic Projects}

\textbf{\href{https://links.arthurdanjou.fr/krum}
  {Krum: Byzantine-Resilient Distributed Learning}} \hfill \textbf{2026}\par
Co-developed a Python/PyTorch research framework combining robust aggregation,
adversarial attacks, published-protocol simulations and reproducible experiment
orchestration.

\vspace{5pt}
\textbf{\href{https://links.arthurdanjou.fr/byzantine-reproduction}
  {Byzantine-Robust Aggregation Reproduction}} \hfill \textbf{2026}\par
Implemented SignGuard, Multi-Krum and MoNNA, with ALIE and Fall of Empires
attacks, from scratch in PyTorch. Documented implementation pitfalls that
informed the design of Krum.

\vspace{5pt}

\textbf{\href{https://links.arthurdanjou.fr/lt-two-layers-neural-networks}
  {Generalisation Bounds for Two-Layer ReLU Networks}} \hfill \textbf{M2}\par
Studied Rademacher complexity bounds using symmetrisation and the positive
homogeneity of ReLU networks in a learning theory coursework project.

\vspace{5pt}
\textbf{\href{https://links.arthurdanjou.fr/dl-dropout-reduces-underfitting}
  {Dropout Reduces Underfitting}} \hfill \textbf{M2}\par
Co-implemented a TensorFlow/Keras reproduction of Liu et al.\ (2023), comparing
early, late, standard and no dropout through learning curves and ablation studies.
